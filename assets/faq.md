# Xpenzi - User Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to **Xpenzi**, your private, intuitive, and feature-packed expense tracker! Below you'll find answers to common questions, tips, and workflow guides.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. How do I add a new transaction?
Tap the large **+** Floating Action Button (FAB) at the bottom of the screen. 
- You can toggle between **Expense**, **Income**, and **Transfer** at the top.
- Set amount, category, account/wallet, title, and date.
- Long-press the **+** button for smart quick-actions!

### 2. How do I sort and filter transactions?
On the **Transactions** tab:
- Tap the **Sort icon** in the top-right header to sort by *Date Created*, *Date Updated*, *Amount*, or *Title*.
- Tap the **Pin icon** (`📌`) to instantly reveal or hide the quick Account Filter bar under the month selector.
- Tap the **Filter icon** to apply complex criteria (categories, tags, date ranges, and income/expense status).

## 🛡️ Offline Intelligence & Auto-Scanning

### 3. How does Offline Notification Reading work?
Xpenzi can automatically capture and parse bank SMS and payment alerts (UPI, debit/credit cards) into transactions on your device.
- Open **Explore > Offline Intelligence** or navigate to **Settings > Xpenzi Intelligence**.
- Enable **Auto-Detect Bank & Payment Alerts** and grant Android notification access.
- Tap **Load Defaults** to install ready-to-use parsing templates for UPI alerts, Debit/Credit SMS, and Card transactions.
- **100% Private**: Your notification data never leaves your device — all processing happens locally using our regex NLP engine.

### 4. How do I view captured notifications and debug templates?
In **Offline Intelligence**, check the **Captured Notification Logs** section. You can inspect raw incoming messages, copy text to your clipboard, and create custom parsing templates tailored to your specific bank.

## 🎨 Customization & Themes

### 5. How do I customize Explore shortcuts?
- Open the **Explore** tab from the bottom navigation.
- Tap the **Edit (✏️)** icon in the top header.
- Drag cards to reorder them in the 2-column grid, or tap the **+** / **-** badges to show or hide shortcuts.
- Tap **Done (✓)** when you're finished.

### 6. How do I change theme and color accents?
Navigate to **Settings > Theme & Style**:
- Choose between **System**, **Light**, **Dark**, and **AMOLED Black** modes.
- On Android, enable **Dynamic Color (Material You)** to match your wallpaper palette, or choose a custom accent color.
- Adjust **Icon Style** between Rounded and Outlined.

### 7. How do I browse category icons?
Go to **Settings > Theme & Style > Category Icon Pack & Gallery** to explore 450+ high-definition category icons with search and category filter tabs (*Food, Shopping, Travel, Bills, etc.*).

## 💾 Backups, Data & Privacy

### 8. How do I backup or export my data?
Navigate to **Settings > Import & Export**:
- **PDF Report**: Export clean, printable PDF financial statements for any date range or account.
- **CSV Spreadsheets**: Export or import standard CSV files compatible with Excel and Google Sheets.
- **Full Database Backups**: Create and restore local encrypted `.json` / `.db` database snapshots, or sync with your private Google Drive.

### 9. Can I migrate data from Cashew?
Yes! Xpenzi has full backward compatibility with Cashew `.json` and `.csv` backup files. Simply use **Settings > Import & Export > Import CSV/DB**.
